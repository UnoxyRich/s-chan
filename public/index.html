Anonymous by designWhat this site keeps

Image conversion and compression happen in your browser; only the finished image is uploaded. S-chan does not store IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, device fingerprints, poster cookies, or account links in its application data. Cloudflare’s zone analytics can expose source IPs and request details to the site operator, and Cloudflare retains sampled HTTP request analytics for at least 31 days. The operator could correlate traffic metadata with post times, so this is not a promise that comments cannot be traced back to a visitor.

Posts stay until automatic capacity pruning removes an older thread. There is no manual deletion tool. Keep personal details out of posts.
