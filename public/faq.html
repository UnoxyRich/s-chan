FAQ

What is S-chan?

S-chan is an anonymous imageboard with one board: /general/. Posts display as Anonymous. There are no accounts or sign-in.

What information does S-chan store?

The app stores post text, public post and thread numbers, timestamps, the sage setting, and image references. It does not store poster IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, device fingerprints, identity cookies, or account-to-post links. Worker observability and invocation logging are disabled.

Cloudflare and network providers still process connection details to deliver the site. Cloudflare zone analytics can expose source IPs and request details to the operator, and sampled HTTP request analytics are retained for at least 31 days. The operator could correlate request times with posts. S-chan cannot promise that a post cannot be traced to a visitor.

What happens to uploaded images?

Images are decoded and converted in your browser with its built-in image and canvas APIs. Drawing the pixels to a fresh canvas removes embedded metadata; the original file is not uploaded. Transparency is preserved.

Supported image formats depend on your browser. Source files can be up to 20 MB; images are resized to a 2,048-pixel longest edge and compressed to at most 2 MB. Animated images become still images. Unsupported or damaged files are rejected.

How do threads work?

Threads sort by bump time. A reply bumps a thread; a reply posted with Sage does not. Threads close after 300 replies. When the board reaches 150 threads, a new thread removes the thread with the oldest bump time and its stored images.

Threads do not expire just because they are old. There is no manual post deletion.

Why did I see a similar-post warning?

S-chan checks text against posts from the previous 24 hours. It warns on exact matches and near matches with at least 85% character 3-gram similarity. Edit your text or choose Post anyway to continue.

How do I quote a post?

Type >>123 with the post number. Select a quote link to open that post.
